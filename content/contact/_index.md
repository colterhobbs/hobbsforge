## Commission Inquiries

If you're asking about a custom piece, please include the following so I can give you an accurate response:

{{% steps %}}

### Describe the piece

What do you want made? Include any reference images or sketches if you have them.

### Intended use

Will it be a working tool, a display piece, a gift? This affects material choices and finish.

### Timeline & budget

Even a rough range helps. Include your location for shipping estimates.
