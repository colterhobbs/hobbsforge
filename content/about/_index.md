Hobbs Tool & Forge is a small garage blacksmith shop focused on practical tools, knives, and functional builds.

This site (and the YouTube channel) documents the process—the work, the mistakes, and the improvements—without the noise.

**No gimmicks. No theatrics.**  
Just fire, steel, and tools built to be used.

### What I Make

- Shop tools: punches, chisels, drifts, hardy tools  
- Functional knives  
- Jigs and fixtures that improve workflow
- Wood and metal jewelry

### Commissions

Limited commissions may be accepted depending on schedule and scope.  
If you have an idea, send a message through the contact form and include:

- What you want made
- Intended use
- Timeline (if any)
- Budget range (even rough)
